# Hero balance + two key-takeaway tiles

## What I understood

1. **The hero copy sits too high.** In the reference, the headline block starts lower and the whole stack is optically centred against the video, with generous room below. Right now the block is vertically centred but reads high because of the header offset, and the bottom of the video is left empty — so the parchment section below shows as an awkward pale band.

2. **Add a takeaway strip at the bottom of the hero**, like the four items in the reference — but only **two**: *Corporate Wellness* and *Become a Practitioner*, each with a lotus-derived line icon, a short label and one supporting line, sitting on the darker foot of the video.

## Changes

**Hero vertical rhythm**
- Increase the stage height slightly and shift the copy block down so it lands in the upper-middle third rather than dead centre, with the eyebrow starting well clear of the nav.
- Rebalance internal spacing (eyebrow -> headline -> subline -> CTAs -> lotus mark) so the stack breathes evenly instead of clustering.
- Deepen the bottom of the scrim so the takeaway row and the join to the next section read as intentional shadow, not a hard edge.

**Takeaway strip**
- A full-width row pinned to the bottom of the hero stage, on a slightly darker translucent band, divided by a hairline in sage at low opacity.
- Two items, each a link:
  - **Corporate Wellness** — "Bring Amoda's coaching and classes to your team." -> /membership
  - **Become a Practitioner** — "Share your practice with a global community." -> /practitioners/apply
- Icons come from the existing lotus/ecosystem linework family (petal-based, 1.5px stroke) in parchment/sage, gold reserved for the centre accent, so nothing new enters the visual language.
- On mobile the two items stack; the row stays inside the hero and keeps the CTAs above it clear.

No copy, menu, video or section-order changes anywhere else.
