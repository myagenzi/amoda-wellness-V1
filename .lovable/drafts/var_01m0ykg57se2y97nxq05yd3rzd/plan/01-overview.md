# Section 3 — "What Amoda Is" as a sticky ecosystem scroller

Today Section 3 is a flat 6-tile card grid on parchment. It becomes a full-bleed
section over a fixed background image, with a sticky left column (eyebrow,
headline, subheadline, feature nav, CTA) and a right column of six cards that
scroll past it. As each card enters view it slides in from the right and the
matching nav item lights up; clicking a nav item scrolls to its card.

All six ecosystem entries keep their approved copy — Trusted Practitioners,
Live Classes & Coaching, A Real Community, The Shoppe, Retreats & Experiences
(waitlist), Corporate Wellness — and each card gets its own photograph in place
of the small line icon.

## Two decisions I'm making inside the existing design system

1. **Six nav items, not dense.** The sticky column uses a two-column grid of
   compact nav pills (label only, `type-label`, hairline border, ink-tinted
   surface). Active pill fills to parchment text on a stronger ink wash;
   inactive sits at reduced opacity. This reads as a quiet index rather than a
   stack of six buttons, and matches the hero takeaway strip's restraint.
2. **Colour comes from tokens, not raw black/white.** The card shells and pills
   use ink washes with a sage hairline and parchment type — the same treatment
   as the hero scrim and takeaway strip — so the section is legible over the
   photograph and stays on-brand in one pass.
